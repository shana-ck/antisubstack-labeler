Alternative Platforms

This is a non-exhaustive list of alternative platforms with various feature offerings in the monetized newsletter space.

  • Beehiiv offers a free plan for up to 2,500 subscribers and can help you migrate your existing Substack publication
  • Buttondown has a free plan for up to 100 subscribers and will offer free migration for existing publications with over 100 subscribers
  • Ghost can be self-hosted or you can use one of their hosting options. It has a neat tool to help you migrate from Substack
  • Kit (formerly ConvertKit) has one of the most generous free tiers, allowing up to 10,000 subscribers. They also will do free migrations
  • Medium is an alternative that hosts blogs and newsletters
  • Memberful has a single pricing tier regardless of size. They can also help you migrate from Substack
  • WordPress is another alternative that allows for self-hosted sites. They can also help you to migrate from Substack

Although not built specifically for blogs and newsletters, Ko-Fi and Patreon are popular among many creators.
